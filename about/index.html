Why Grey Horse

Search works better when context comes first.

The hardest technical searches are rarely solved by finding more people with the right title. They start with understanding the work itself: what the person will need to build, own or change, how the team is structured, and which parts of the market are most likely to produce the right profile.

That matters particularly across software, hardware and robotics, where strong candidates often sit between disciplines or use different titles for similar work. Grey Horse starts with the problem, then expands into the companies, adjacent talent pools and technical backgrounds most relevant to solving it.

The goal is not a larger candidate list. It is a better-calibrated search.

Search Principles

The principles behind the work.

01 / 03
01

Start with the work.

Before defining the candidate market, get clear on what the person actually needs to build, own and solve. Job titles help describe a search. They should not determine it.

02

Search beyond obvious titles.

Technical talent does not always organize itself neatly around company categories or job titles. The right person may sit in an adjacent market, work across disciplines, or describe similar experience differently.

03

Keep the market close to the decision.

Candidate response, client feedback and market learning should continuously inform the search. When the market shows something different from the original assumption, the search should adapt.
