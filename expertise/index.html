Where We Get Involved

Expertise matters most when the profile is not obvious.

Grey Horse is most useful when a company needs more than access to candidates: sharper calibration, a more specialized market, or a better understanding of where the right talent may be sitting.

01

When an early hire will shape what comes next.

Founding and early technical hires can influence product architecture, engineering standards, hiring quality and the shape of the team that follows. The search needs to account for both the immediate role and what the company is becoming.

02

When the talent market is narrow or difficult to map.

Some roles require experience that exists across several industries, titles or technical disciplines. Grey Horse helps define the relevant market, identify adjacent talent pools and focus the search on the experience that matches the actual problem.

03

When getting the leadership hire wrong is expensive.

A senior technical hire can influence product direction, team quality and the ability to scale. These searches require judgment around company stage, technical context and the kind of leader the business actually needs.

04

When one difficult search becomes a broader hiring plan.

As technical teams grow, several searches can begin to overlap. Understanding the market across those roles helps keep calibration consistent and avoids treating every opening as a completely separate problem.
